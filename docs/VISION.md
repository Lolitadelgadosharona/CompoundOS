# Vision

## Overview

CompoundOS is an AI Family Office and Wealth Operating System designed to protect capital, encourage long-term compounding, enforce disciplined decision-making, and provide explainable decision support.

## Guiding Principles

1. Protect capital.
2. Maximize long-term compounding.
3. Enforce investment discipline over market prediction.
4. Provide explainable decision support rather than autonomous trading.

## Sprint 001 Scope

This sprint establishes the repository foundation, documentation, development workflow, and minimal health endpoints only.
