# Product Requirements Document

## Status

Draft for Sprint 001 only.

## Summary

CompoundOS will begin as a documented, explainable operating system for family office decision support. The initial sprint focuses on project foundation rather than product functionality.

## Objectives

- Create a scalable monorepo structure.
- Establish development and validation standards.
- Define an architectural and governance foundation.

## Non-Goals for Sprint 001

- Trading execution
- Broker integrations
- Autonomous agents
- Authentication
- Investment recommendations
