## Objective

After thirteen sprints and 318 tests, CompoundOS is a comprehensive
AI family office platform. Sprint 023 optimizes for real-world daily
operation: live household integration, AI calibration improvement,
investor behavior awareness, and long-term wealth planning tools.

---

## Slice A — Live Household Operation

### Goal
Make the household financial dashboard genuinely useful for daily use.

### Tasks
- Real-time net worth tracking (connected accounts — manual update)
- Cash flow monitoring (income, expenses, savings rate)
- Bill and subscription tracking (recurring obligations)
- Emergency fund status (3-6 months expenses)
- Tax estimate (quarterly estimated tax projection)

### Deliverable
Enhanced /dashboard with live household metrics

---

## Slice B — AI Calibration Improvement

### Goal
Close the loop between AI predictions and real outcomes.

### Tasks
- Automated accuracy reports (weekly digest)
- Per-perspective calibration (which perspective is most reliable?)
- Confidence band refinement (tighten ±10 → ±5 over time)
- Historical analysis: "AI would have recommended X, actual outcome was Y"
- Prompt tuning based on accuracy data

### Deliverable
Weekly calibration report + tuning recommendations

---

## Slice C — Investor Behavior Layer

### Goal
Help the Owner understand their own decision patterns.

### Insights
- Action bias: do you approve too many BUY recommendations?
- Decision latency: how long between research and decision?
- Regret tracking: decisions you later reversed
- Pattern detection: "You tend to approve tech, reject healthcare"
- Risk appetite tracking over time

### Constraints
Informational only. Never judgmental. Owner data is private.

---

## Slice D — Long-Term Wealth Planning

### Goal
Shift perspective from individual investments to long-term goals.

### Tools
- Retirement projection (compound growth calculator)
- College funding planner (529 analysis)
- Estate planning checklist (informational)
- Generational wealth transfer basics
- Charitable giving optimization (DAF, QCD basics)

### Constraints
Not financial advice. Educational only. No specific recommendations.

---

## Constraints

- AI advisory only
- Owner final authority
- No broker
- No trading
- No autonomous investment execution

---

## Owner Decisions Required

6-8 decisions covering:
- Household data sources and update frequency
- Calibration report format and frequency
- Behavior insight depth (how much self-reflection?)
- Long-term planning scope (retirement only or multi-goal?)
